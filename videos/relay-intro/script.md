# anyr relay intro, voice-over script (target 130-145 words)

1. Hook. Your Mac already runs good models.
2. Problem. But they are stuck on one machine, behind a different API. Your apps can't reach them.
3. Command. AnyRouter fixes that with one command. anyr relay start.
4. Anatomy. Your app calls AnyRouter with the key you already use. The edge finds your Mac through a secure tunnel your Mac opened itself, so no ports are open. Apple's Foundation Model, Perplexity's Decider, Ollama or LM Studio answers, and the reply streams back. And it is only you. Your Mac answers your requests, nobody else's.
5. Compare. In the playground, put a Decider on your Mac next to a hosted decision model like Jev, and see both answers and their timing side by side.
6. CTA. Update and start. anyr upgrade, then anyr relay start. anyrouter dot dev.
