## Summary

Four days, one neighbourhood per day, and no more than two sights before lunch. Trains are short, so the plan walks wherever it can.

## Day 1: Higashiyama

Start at Kiyomizu-dera before 8:00, then walk down Sannenzaka and Ninenzaka while the lanes are quiet. Lunch near Yasaka Shrine. Spend the afternoon in Maruyama Park and finish at Gion at dusk.

## Day 2: Arashiyama

Take the early JR train. See the bamboo grove first, then Tenryu-ji garden when it opens. Rent a bike for the river path and stop at Okochi Sanso for matcha.

## Day 3: Northern temples

Kinkaku-ji in the morning, Ryoan-ji rock garden after, then Ninna-ji. Each is a short bus ride from the last.

## Day 4: Markets and gardens

Nishiki Market for breakfast. Walk the Philosopher's Path to Nanzen-ji, then rest in the Murin-an garden before the evening train.

## What to pack

- Shoes you can slip off at temple doors
- A small towel and a coin purse
- An IC card for buses and trains
