# Artifacts, 7 daily episodes

Each is 20 to 26 s, one capability, 9:16 (1080x1920) plus a 16:9 cut (1920x1080), captions burned in, same audio logo at the end. Links in captions go to the episode's demo artifact (made-up data, unlisted). Post one a day, in this order.

| Day | File | Capability | Hook (on screen in the first 1.5 s) | Length |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ep1 | `anyr artifact publish` prints a link | "One link." | 26.3 s |
| 2 | ep2 | `.doc.md` becomes a document with an outline | "A document, not a text file." | 24.4 s |
| 3 | ep3 | `.slides.md` becomes a 16:9 deck | "Slides from a markdown file." | 22.5 s |
| 4 | ep4 | `.mmd` becomes a diagram, drawn in the browser | "A diagram, from plain text." | 20.6 s |
| 5 | ep5 | One React file becomes a live component | "Not just pages. Live components." | 24.4 s |
| 6 | ep6 | Publish again with `--id`: new version, same URL | "Same link." | 22.5 s |
| 7 | ep7 | Private by default, `unlisted`, `public` | the three visibility cards | 20.6 s |
