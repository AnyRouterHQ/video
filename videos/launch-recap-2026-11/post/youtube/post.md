AnyRouter turns 3 months, in numbers
AnyRouter is one API key for every model. anybot, our AI agent fleet, builds it.

Users
- 15,034 users all time, up from 1,695 on Aug 4
- 5,966 people used AnyRouter in the last 30 days (Oct 6 to Nov 4). The busiest day, Oct 26, had 2,288.

Models
- 323 models are live today
- We added 220 of them between Aug 5 and Nov 4
- Our catalog pipeline found at least 56 of those on its own (traced to monitor tickets and catalog jobs)

Traffic, last 30 days
- 1.3M requests and 65B tokens

Building it, Aug 5 to Nov 4
- anybot landed 4,506 commits and merged 3,435 pull requests
- 2,278 of those commits name an AI co-author (counted from co-author trailers)
- anybot shipped 753 deploys, with 59 on the busiest day
